Modeling Accessibility: Optimizing Oil Palm Supply Chains

Mon 15 Dec 2025 • 16:19 GMT by Spatiality

Field Measuring & Data Collecting. Documentation by Spatiality

Effective accessibility modeling serves as a cornerstone for sustainable supply chain management in the palm oil industry. By quantifying the time and ease required to reach processing facilities (mills and ports), stakeholders can move beyond simple distance metrics to understand operational efficiency. A robust accessibility model—adapted from the framework established by Pozzi, Robinson, and Nelson—integrates complex variables like road network quality, land cover, and topography to estimate travel time. In this context, "good accessibility" is essential for minimizing post-harvest quality degradation of Fresh Fruit Bunches (FFB) and optimizing transportation logistics for both smallholders and industrial producers.

Our adapted approach refines traditional accessibility mapping by incorporating highly specific road speed calculations relevant to local conditions and integrating diverse, multi-source datasets—ranging from Badan Informasi Geospasial (BIG) road networks and OpenStreetMap river data to Copernicus land cover classification. By utilizing an Analytical Hierarchy Process (AHP) alongside accessibility modeling, we can overlay environmental constraints such as peatland distribution, forest degradation status, and forest utilization zones. This methodology allows for a sophisticated "supply catchment" analysis, identifying areas that are both logistically viable and aligned with No Deforestation, No Peat, and No Exploitation (NDPE) regulations.

Analysis Workflow

Workflow Comparison. Documentation by Spatiality

The practical outputs of this model are designed to provide actionable intelligence for decision-makers:

  • Mill Catchment Mapping: Defines areas within a 3-hour travel time to specific processing facilities, identifying primary supply zones.
  • Competitor Overlap: Visualizes catchment intersections to identify regions where supply zones compete, offering insights into market dynamics.
  • Compliance Verification: Pinpoints the potential volume of smallholder and industrial palm oil that remains eligible for international markets by adhering to NDPE standards.
  • Strategic Buffering: Provides detailed assessments of mills and concessions located within critical supply corridors, such as the 50km buffers along the Mahakam, Telen, and Kedang Kepala rivers.
