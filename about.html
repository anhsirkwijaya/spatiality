About Me

With over seven years of experience as a specialized GIS Analyst in the Government and NGO sector, I serve as a "wingman" for many strategic divisions navigating the complexities of sustainable land management. My work is defined by the belief that spatial data is most powerful when it is accessible and actionable. From developing an 8-province thematic atlas to conducting deep-dive hydrological and supply chain analyses for oil palm landscapes, I provide the technical clarity needed to transform large-scale environmental challenges into structured, data-driven solutions.

I am a strong advocate for "force multiplier" technology—building tools that allow teams to work smarter, not harder. By engineering custom automation scripts in ArcGIS and Google Earth Engine, I have successfully transitioned manual, time-intensive workflows into streamlined digital systems. To ensure these innovations stick, I prioritize knowledge transfer; I’ve authored comprehensive technical manuals and produced a library of video tutorials on Avenza Maps and data hygiene, ensuring that field staff and program teams are fully equipped to maintain data integrity in any environment.

Beyond the screen, my impact is measured by the empowerment of those on the ground. I have a proven track record of bringing GIS directly to the field, facilitating participatory mapping sessions where local farmers document their own land boundaries. By teaching basic spatial literacy to farmer communities, I bridge the gap between high-level satellite monitoring and real-world land rights. My goal is to continue building the spatial infrastructure that supports regenerative agriculture, ensuring that every map produced serves as a catalyst for sustainable community growth.

Background

Graduated from Faculty of Geography at Universitas Gadjah Mada (2013-2018) after completed bachelor thesis with specific topic in Groundwater Vulnerability Assessment. Earned Bachelor of Science in environmental geography, majoring land and water resource. I was a gamelan player and reporter of Bulaksumur student newspaper. I also contribute to social project of “Jambanisasi Program” in Munggu Village, Central Bangka on 2015. On 2017, I brought this GIS-based activity to advanced in the first National GIS Competition, and come as the champion, implementing binary method on site selection development analysis. Though I didn’t continue to pursue to higher education, I keep learning and develop my skillset through certified courses, webinar, and self-learning to keep align with present knowledge and technology.
